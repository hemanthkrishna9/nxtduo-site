Publik

Every park, lake and library in Bengaluru.

A free guide to about 1,880 public spaces in Bengaluru: parks, playgrounds, lakes, libraries and public toilets, with timings, directions, "open now" and "near me". Visitors can add photos and notes, and flag a place that is under repair.

What it does

  • Find a placeSearch or filter parks, playgrounds, lakes, libraries and public toilets across Bengaluru.
  • Open now, near meSee what is open right now and what is closest, on a list or a map.
  • Before you goTimings, directions, and short tips from people who went, including playground safety.
  • Add a photo or a noteShare a photo or a short note about a place, so the next visitor knows what to expect.
  • Flag a problemReport a place that is under repair. No account, nothing about you is stored.

Where the data comes from

The list combines the BBMP park list (via OpenCity), OpenStreetMap, and publicly listed opening hours. Places change, so the tips and reports from visitors help keep it honest.

Who it is for

Parents looking for a playground, walkers looking for a quiet lake, students looking for a library, and anyone who needs a public toilet in a hurry. It is a website, so it works on any phone with nothing to install.

Questions

Is Publik free?

Yes. It is a free website at publik.nxtduo.com, with no sign-in.

How many places are in Publik?

About 1,880 public spaces in Bengaluru: parks, playgrounds, lakes, libraries and public toilets.

Does it track my location?

No. "Near me" uses your location only inside your browser to sort places. It is never sent to us.

A place is wrong. What do I do?

Use the report buttons on the place, or write to support@nxtduo.com.
