What it does

  • Reminders that arrive early30, 15, 7 and 3 days before, and on the day, by push and email. Recurring renewals roll forward with one tap.
  • Documents kept with the reminderAttach the policy PDF, the warranty card or a photo of the receipt, so the paperwork is there when you need to claim.
  • Share with familyShare one reminder, not your whole account. Everyone sees what is due.
  • Built for patchy networksAdd reminders offline; they sync later. Lock the app with Face ID or your fingerprint.

Who it is for

Anyone in India who has ever paid a late fee, driven with an expired PUC, or found a forgotten subscription on a card statement. We built it for our own family first, because we kept missing renewals.

How to get it

The iPhone app is in App Store review and the Android app is in closed testing on Google Play. There is a free plan and an optional Premium plan. Plans and updates are on nxtdue.com.

Questions

Is NxtDue free?

There is a free plan. Premium adds more; see nxtdue.com for what each plan includes.

Where is my data stored?

On servers in India. NxtDue shows no ads and does not sell your data.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can export or delete everything yourself in the app's Settings.
