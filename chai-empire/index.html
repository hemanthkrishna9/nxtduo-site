Chai Empire

From one tapri to a chain of hotels.

A cosy 3D tycoon game. Start as a chaiwala kid with one roadside tapri, brew masala chai, serve customers before their patience runs out, and grow it into a chain of hotels. In English, Hindi and Telugu.

How it plays

There are no menus to dig through. You walk the kid around the stall and stand in glowing rings to make things happen:

  • CollectMilk from the cow and leaves from the tea bush.
  • BrewChai on the stove, and stock the counter with kulhads.
  • ServeCustomers before their patience runs out. Fast service earns tips.
  • GrowA second cow, a bench, a masala garden, and one day, hotels.

Who it is for

Players who like short, relaxing tycoon games, and anyone who has stood at a roadside chai stall. The language button switches between English, Hindi and Telugu.

Status

The first stage is playable from start to finish, and we are polishing it. It is not in any store yet. If you want to try it early, write to us.
