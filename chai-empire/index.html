Chai Empire From one tapri to a chain of hotels.

A cosy 3D tycoon game. You start as a chaiwala kid with one roadside tapri, brew masala chai, and grow it into a chain of hotels. In English, Hindi and Telugu.

Tell us if you want early access →

Chai Empire is a playable prototype. It is not released yet.

What it does

There are no menus to dig through. You walk the kid around the stall and stand in glowing rings to make things happen:

  • Collect milk from the cow and leaves from the tea bush.
  • Brew chai on the stove and stock the counter with kulhads.
  • Serve customers before their patience runs out, then collect the cash. Fast service earns tips.
  • Spend coins on new stations: a second cow, a bench, a masala garden for masala chai.

Who it is for

Players who like short, relaxing idle and tycoon games, and anyone who has stood at a roadside chai stall. You play with the keyboard or by dragging a finger on a phone, and the language button switches between English, Hindi and Telugu.

Status

The first stage is playable from start to finish, and we are polishing it. It is not in any store yet. If you want to try it early, write to us.
