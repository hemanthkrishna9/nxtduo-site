FableFox

Little ears. Big dreams.

Indian bedtime stories told in a parent's own voice. Read two short lines once, and FableFox tells stories from the Ramayana, the Panchatantra and Tenali Raman in your voice, calling your child by the pet name you use at home.

How it works

  1. Choose your language, agree to the consent, and type your child's pet name.
  2. Read two short lines on screen, about 6 to 20 seconds.
  3. Hear a short preview in your voice. If it sounds like you, FableFox makes the stories.
  4. Each story downloads once and plays offline. At bedtime the player locks the screen against small hands and fades out at the end.

Your voice stays yours

Only the person whose voice it is can agree to record it. Your voice makes stories for your family only and is never shared. You can delete your voice and every story at any time from the app. Read the privacy policy.

Who it is for

Parents who travel, work late or live apart from their children for a while, and grandparents who want their voice at bedtime. Also any family that wants the old stories told in their own language.

Questions

A test version is with a small group of families. Email support@nxtduo.com to ask to join.

Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil and Malayalam.

Do the stories need the internet?

Only to download them once. After that they play offline.
