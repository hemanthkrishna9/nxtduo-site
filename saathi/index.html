A diary that listens, and stays on your phone.

What it does

You write to Saathi the way you would write a diary. It replies in a few short, warm lines, like a friend who remembers your life. It can bring up the right earlier thing at the right moment, such as asking how Thursday's interview went.

You stay in charge of what it remembers. Before it keeps a new fact, it asks. You can see, edit or delete your memories.

Private by design

The AI model runs on the phone itself. After a one-time model download, Saathi works with no internet at all. Your entries are encrypted on the phone, there is no account, and there is no cloud copy of your diary. If someone writes that they are in danger, safety comes first: it shows real helplines for their country.

Questions

Does Saathi send my diary to the cloud?

No. The AI runs on your phone, and your diary stays there, encrypted.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the AI model (about 3.5 GB). After that it works offline.

Can I use Saathi today?

It is in testing. Email support@nxtduo.com to join.
