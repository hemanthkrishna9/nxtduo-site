Saathi A diary that listens, and stays on your phone.

A journaling companion that runs entirely on your own device. It talks in English, Hinglish or Tenglish, and never sends your diary to the cloud.

Saathi is a research project. There is no app to download yet.

🌼Saathi

What it does

You write to Saathi the way you would write a diary. It replies in a few short, warm lines, like a friend who remembers your life. It can bring up the right earlier thing at the right moment, such as asking how Thursday's interview went.

You stay in charge of what it remembers. Before it keeps a new fact, it asks. You can see, edit or delete your memories.

Private by design

The AI model runs on the phone itself. Your entries are not sent to a server, and there is no cloud copy of your diary. Saathi is honest that it is an AI. If someone writes that they are in danger, safety comes first: it points them to real help, such as the Tele-MANAS helpline in India.

Status

Saathi is in research. We are testing it on a laptop prototype before building the phone app. If you want to hear when it is ready, write to us.

Questions

Does Saathi send my diary to the cloud?

No. Saathi is designed to run entirely on your own device, so your diary stays with you.

Can I use Saathi today?

Not yet. Saathi is a research project. Email support@nxtduo.com if you want early access.
