What it does

The map is a slice of real central Bengaluru from OpenStreetMap: MG Road, Brigade Road, Church Street, Cubbon Park, Vidhana Soudha and the purple metro line. You hail a passenger, agree the fare, and race the clock through traffic where nobody follows the rules.

  • Real roadsPotholes, speed humps and monsoon rain that floods the road.
  • Night shiftsNight falls during your shift, and the streetlights flicker.
  • ChallansRed-light cameras and e-challans for bad driving.
  • LeaderboardsSign in if you want to put your best shift on the board.

How to play

Open meter-mele.pages.dev in a browser. On a phone you steer by dragging, or switch to tilt or buttons. You can install it as an app from the browser, and after the first load it also works offline.

Who it is for

Anyone who has ridden an auto in Bengaluru and heard "meter mele" (the name means "above the meter"). Locals spot their own streets. Everyone else gets a quick, silly, very local driving game.

Questions

Is Meter Mele free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser at meter-mele.pages.dev.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on phones and laptops. You can install it as an app from the browser if you like.

Are the streets real?

Yes. The roads, buildings and landmarks come from OpenStreetMap data of central Bengaluru.
