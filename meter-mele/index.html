Meter Mele ಮೀಟರ್ ಮೇಲೆ Beat the meter across real Bengaluru.

Drive an auto through streets built from real map data. Pick up passengers and get them there before the meter beats you. Potholes, rain, night rides and leaderboards. It runs in the browser on any phone or laptop, with nothing to install.

Play Meter Mele now →

Free, in your browser, at meter-mele.pages.dev.

What it does

The map is a slice of real central Bengaluru from OpenStreetMap: MG Road, Brigade Road, Church Street, Cubbon Park, Vidhana Soudha and the purple metro line. You hail a passenger, agree the fare, and race the clock through traffic where nobody follows the rules.

  • Potholes, speed humps and monsoon rain that floods the road.
  • Night falls during your shift, and the streetlights flicker.
  • Red-light cameras and e-challans for bad driving.
  • Leaderboards to compare your best shift with friends.

Who it is for

Anyone who has ridden an auto in Bengaluru and heard "meter mele" (the name means "above the meter"). Locals spot their own streets. Everyone else gets a quick, silly, very local driving game.

How to play

Open meter-mele.pages.dev in a browser. On a phone you steer by dragging, or you can switch to tilt or buttons. You can install it as an app from the browser, and after the first load it also works offline.

Questions

Is Meter Mele free?

Yes. Meter Mele is free to play in your browser at meter-mele.pages.dev.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on phones and laptops. If you like, you can install it as an app from the browser.

Are the streets real?

Yes. The roads, buildings and landmarks come from OpenStreetMap data of central Bengaluru.
