The full policy

PassOn's complete privacy policy is on the PassOn website: passon.nxtduo.com/privacy. It is the version that applies. This page is a short summary of it.

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.
  • Ticket and request details you type are public. Your mobile number stays private: people chat with you on PassOn without seeing it, unless you choose to show or share it.
  • Booking IDs and PNRs are stored only as a one-way code (a hash), to stop the same ticket being listed twice.
  • The ticket photo and your UPI ID are shown only to the one person you give the ticket to.
  • Chats can be read only by the two people in them. PassOn staff read a chat only if one of you reports it.
  • Deletion: everything about a listing, request or chat is deleted 7 days after the event. Reported items keep a small safety record for 180 days.
  • Cloudflare hosts PassOn and stores the data. We do not sell or share your data.

Contact

To ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, write to support@nxtduo.com. PassOn is not for children under 18.
