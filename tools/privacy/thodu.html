The short version

Thodu is a concept. Nothing is built or released yet, so there is no app or service collecting data.

If you write to us

If you email us about Thodu, we keep your email address and message only to reply and to tell you when there is something to try. We never share them, and we delete them when you ask.

Before we build it

Thodu would make phone calls to parents and summarise them for their children, which means handling voices, phone numbers and health details with great care. Before anything is released, we will publish a full policy here explaining exactly what is collected, how consent from both the parent and the child works, and how to delete it.
