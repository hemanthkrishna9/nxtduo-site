The short version

Chai Empire is a playable prototype and is not released yet. The prototype has no account, no ads, no analytics and no online features, and it sends no personal data to us. Your progress and language choice are saved only on your own device.

Early access

If you write to us for early access, we keep your email address and message only to reply and to send you the test version. We delete them when you ask, or when early access ends.

Before release

If the released game adds anything that handles personal data, such as cloud saves, leaderboards or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is out, and the date at the top will change.
