The studio

Two people. One studio.

NxtDuo is a small, independent studio making apps for families everywhere. We build things we want for our own family first: a reminder app because we kept missing renewals, bedtime stories for when we are away, a brushing coach for the kids.

With great power comes great responsibility.

  • 01
    Kind by default.No dark patterns, no guilt trips, no selling your data.
  • 02
    Made for the world.Your language, your city, your everyday life, wherever you are.
  • 03
    Honest about status.Live means live. Concepts are called concepts.
  • 04
    Small and careful.Fewer features, done properly.
