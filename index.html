Saathi · సాథీ · education

A tutor in every pocket.
No internet needed.

Crores of students in small towns and villages study on shared, low-cost phones with patchy data. Saathi is an AI tutor that lives on the phone itself. It explains, quizzes and encourages in Telugu, Hindi or English, and it remembers each student.

  • Remembers the child. Saathi recalls that fractions were hard last week and comes back to them, like a good teacher does.
  • Works fully offline. The AI model runs on the phone. No data pack, no signal, no cost per question.
  • Private by design. A child's questions and mistakes never leave the device.
  • Safe for young minds. Built-in checks catch distress in three languages and point to real help, such as Tele-MANAS.
  • Working prototype: on-device memory engine
  • Next: exam tutor pilot

Thodu · తోడు · relationships

Far from home.
Never out of touch.

దూరంగా ఉన్నా, దగ్గరగా.

Millions of Indian children live in another city or another country from their parents. Parents say "all is fine" and hide the rest. Thodu phones amma and nanna on a normal phone, in Telugu, asks what you would ask, and sends you a short, honest note. Then Claude, Anthropic's AI, looks across weeks of calls to understand the relationship, not just the day.

  • A bridge, never a replacement. Every call is made on your behalf: "Namaskaram Amma, Ravi tarapuna call chestunna."
  • Catches what gets hidden. A missed tablet, a "gas" pain that sounds like the chest, a doctor visit skipped.
  • No smartphone needed. Parents just pick up the phone. Nothing to install, nothing to learn.
  • Understands the relationship. Claude reads each call alongside the ones before it and notices slow changes: a parent who sounds lonelier, a pain that keeps coming back, a topic they avoid.
  • Your calls become about love, not "did you eat?" Thodu suggests what to talk about next.
  • Being built on the Claude API
  • Designed: 67 failure cases mapped

How it works

One engine. Built for India first.

Saathi and Thodu share the same core: an AI that remembers, speaks local languages and is careful with people.

01

Memory that matters

Our memory engine picks the one right thing to bring up: a weak topic, an upcoming exam, a parent's doctor visit. It skips what would hurt or nag.

02

Small phones, big models

Saathi runs a compact AI model on the phone itself. Thodu is designed around Claude in the cloud, to plan calls and understand each family over time. Claude also helps us write and check Saathi's lessons.

03

Safety in every language

Crisis and urgent-health checks in English, Hindi and Telugu, with the right helpline for the country. Tested on multi-day conversations, not one-line demos.

Built with Claude

Claude is at the heart of how we build and what we build.

Our users are children and elderly parents. We need an AI that is careful, honest and follows instructions, so we chose Claude.

Thodu

Claude API: understanding families

Claude plans each Telugu check-in call, reads it alongside weeks of earlier calls, flags warning signs and notices slow changes in a parent's health and mood. It then writes the child a short, kind, honest note.

Saathi

Claude API: lessons that ship offline

Claude writes and reviews question banks and step-by-step explanations in Telugu, Hindi and English. Every lesson is checked before it ships to the phone, where the student studies with no internet.

Team

Claude Code: two people, shipping like ten

We build with Claude Code every day: the offline memory engine, the safety checks in three languages, 240 automated tests and this website.
