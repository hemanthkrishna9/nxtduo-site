Arunachala

Talk with Ramana.

A free chat app on the teachings of Sri Ramana Maharshi. It answers in his calm style, using only passages from his recorded words in 10 books, and each answer names its source book. It says it is an AI if you ask, and if someone is in distress it shows the Tele-MANAS helpline, 14416.
