About us

Two people. One studio.

NxtDuo is a small, independent studio from India. We build things we want for our own family first: a reminder app because we kept missing renewals, a game about the autos we ride every day, a brushing coach for the kids.

  • Kind by default. No dark patterns, no guilt trips, no selling your data.
  • Made for India. Local languages, local streets, local life.
  • Small and careful. Fewer features, done properly.
