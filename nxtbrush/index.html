NxtBrush Brush every zone, every time.

Guided, animated toothbrushing for ages 3 to 80. A mouth map shows the next zone, a timer counts down, a chime says when to move on. It works offline, with any toothbrush.

Tell us if you want early access →

NxtBrush is in development. It is not released yet.

What it does

You stand at the sink and tap Start. The phone does the rest:

  • A mouth map lights up the zone to brush next.
  • A short animation shows the motion for that zone.
  • A timer ring counts down, and every zone change is shown, heard (a soft chime) and felt (a vibration), so you can follow it with your eyes half-closed.

Optional teeth-check photos can help the app give more time to the spots that need it. This is coaching, never a diagnosis. For pain or bleeding that lasts, see a dentist.

Who it is for

Families. The look changes with age: playful for small children, a game for older kids, and calm with bigger text for teens, adults and older people. Streaks and small rewards help the habit, with no guilt and no dark patterns. We built it as a brushing coach for our own kids.

Status

NxtBrush is in development and not in the stores yet. If you want to try it early, write to us.

Questions

Is NxtBrush available now?

Not yet. It is in development. Email support@nxtduo.com if you want early access.

Does NxtBrush need the internet?

No. Brushing works offline. Only the optional photo check needs a connection.

Is NxtBrush medical advice?

No. It gives brushing coaching, not a diagnosis. See a dentist for pain, bleeding that lasts, or anything unusual.
