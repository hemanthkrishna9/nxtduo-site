NxtBrush

Brush every zone, every time.

Guided, animated toothbrushing for ages 3 to 80. A mouth map shows the next zone, a timer counts down, a chime says when to move on. It works offline, with any toothbrush.

What it does

You stand at the sink and tap Start. The phone does the rest:

  • Mouth mapLights up the zone to brush next, with a short animation of the motion.
  • See, hear, feelA timer ring, a soft chime and a gentle vibration at every zone change.
  • Kind streaksSmall rewards and a family board, with a streak freeze and no guilt.
  • Teeth checkOptional photos help it give more time to the spots that need it. Coaching, never a diagnosis.

Who it is for

Families. The look changes with age: playful for small children, a game for older kids, and calm with bigger text for teens, adults and older people. We built it as a brushing coach for our own kids.

Status

NxtBrush is in development and not in the stores yet. If you want to try it early, write to us.

Questions

Does NxtBrush need the internet?

No. Brushing works offline. Only the optional teeth check needs a connection.

Is it medical advice?

No. It gives brushing coaching, not a diagnosis. See a dentist for pain, bleeding that lasts, or anything unusual.

Does it need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and no ads.
